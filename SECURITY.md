# Security Policy

## Portfolio Website Security Statement

This repository hosts the official professional portfolio website of **Owino Brian Otieno**.

The website is intended for public viewing of professional information, research summaries, publications, certifications, projects, and academic achievements.

---

## Research Materials Protection

The portfolio may reference:

- Research Papers
- Academic Publications
- Dissertations
- Technical Reports
- Articles
- Essays
- Assignments
- Project Documentation

These materials remain the intellectual property of their respective authors and are not publicly distributed without permission.

Access to full research resources may require direct authorization from the owner.

---

## Responsible Use

Visitors may:

- View portfolio content
- Review public project descriptions
- Read publication summaries
- Access publicly shared resources

Visitors may not:

- Reproduce copyrighted material without permission
- Misrepresent authorship of research work
- Redistribute protected documents
- Attempt unauthorized access to private resources
